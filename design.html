Design System & Style Guide

Color Palette

Primary — Trustworthy Blue

Primary actions, links, focus states. 600 for buttons, 500 for accents, 50-100 for backgrounds.

Success — Income Green

Positive amounts, income entries, success states. 600 for buttons, 100/50 for badges.

Warning — Attention Amber

Pending states, gift category, attention needed.

Error — Danger Red

Destructive actions, validation errors, delete confirmations.

Pink — Rose

Accent color for highlights, new features, promotional elements, and optional category.

Neutral — Slate

Text, borders, backgrounds. 900/50 for text, 200/300 for borders, 50/100/900/950 for surfaces.

Used for category badges, chart segments, and entry row accents.
